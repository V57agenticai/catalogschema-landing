Capabilities

Three controls. One catalog.

Each capability is a merchant action. Nothing in this set publishes product copy on its own.

AI Text Elevation

Draft-first, with a 250-word ceiling. CatalogSchema generates structured product copy and holds it for Review & Publish before it can go live.

The merchant reviews the draft, edits it if needed, and publishes only the version they approve.

Zero-Code JSON-LD Schema

Schema is injected through Shopify App Embed blocks. No Liquid edits.

The structured data covers product and offer fields, including price and stock or availability. It does not claim review or rating markup.

Operation-Based Credits

1 operation = 1 credit. An operation is one billable action, such as an AI elevation, a schema injection, or a SKU assignment.

One store keeps a single active dashboard tab for those actions, so two open tabs do not run the same work at once. Review & Publish is free. Credits never expire while the app is active.

How it works

Draft-first and controlled

The live listing changes when the merchant publishes. Batch work stays in the tab that holds the store.

  1. Elevate the draft

    AI text elevation writes structured copy up to 250 words. The result stays a draft.

  2. Inject schema

    JSON-LD goes out through the App Embed, with price and availability. Theme code stays untouched.

  3. Spend by operation

    Each elevation, schema injection, or SKU assignment is one credit. A product or a batch can run several. One active tab owns the run.

  4. Review & Publish

    Approval is the live step. It does not cost a credit. Until then, the elevated description is not the storefront copy.

FAQ & Support

What does one credit buy?

1 operation = 1 credit. An operation is one billable action, such as an AI elevation, a schema injection, or a SKU assignment. A product or a batch can use more than one credit. Review & Publish does not cost a credit. Credits never expire while the app is active.

When does elevated copy go live?

It stays a draft, capped at 250 words, until the merchant approves it with Review & Publish.

What does schema include?

JSON-LD is injected through the Shopify App Embed. It carries structured product and offer data, including price and stock or availability. It does not include review or rating fields.

Can two dashboard tabs run at once?

One active tab holds Elevate, Inject Schema, and Process All for that store. A second tab waits until it takes control or the first tab’s lease ends.

Contact

Email support@catalogschema.com. If a system error stops an elevation before it completes, write that address and include the store domain.

Privacy Policy

CatalogSchema provides draft AI product copy, JSON-LD injection, and identifier tools to merchants who install the app on a Shopify store. Questions go to support@catalogschema.com.

Shopify API and GDPR

The app uses the Shopify Admin API with the scopes granted at install. Shopify sends the mandatory compliance webhooks customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact. Customer topics are acknowledged. shop/redact deletes stored shop data.

Store and catalog data

CatalogSchema processes the shop domain, the session required to read and update that store, and product metadata and catalog fields used for elevation, schema, and SKU work. Those fields include title, description, vendor, product type, tags, variants, price, availability, and identifiers.

Buyer personal data

The app does not collect buyer personal information. It does not use a customer’s order history, email, or address as an input to elevation or schema.

AI processing

Product text is sent to the configured model to produce a draft. CatalogSchema does not use merchant catalogs to train public AI models.

Uninstall and shop/redact

Credits never expire while the app remains installed. After uninstall, Shopify shop/redact removes stored usage records in shopify_copy_usage and credit purchase records in shopify_credit_purchases.

Terms of Service

These terms cover use of CatalogSchema by the merchant who installs it on a Shopify store. Installing or continuing to use the app means the merchant accepts them. Contact support@catalogschema.com with questions.

The service

CatalogSchema drafts product copy, injects JSON-LD through the Shopify App Embed, and can assign SKUs. Elevated copy stays a draft until the merchant uses Review & Publish. The merchant is responsible for the copy they approve and for the products they mark in or out of a run.

Credits

1 operation = 1 credit. An operation is one billable action, such as an AI elevation, schema injection, or SKU assignment. Products and batches may consume multiple credits. Review & Publish is free. Credits never expire while the app is active. Credit packs are purchased through Shopify Billing.

Availability

The service depends on Shopify and on the third-party model used for drafts. Rate limits, outages, and API changes can delay or stop a run. Completed operations are not reversed by a later outage.

Ending use

The merchant may uninstall at any time from Shopify admin. Shopify shop/redact then removes stored usage in shopify_copy_usage and credit purchase records in shopify_credit_purchases.

  2. CatalogSchema does not guarantee search rankings, rich-snippet display, or a lift in traffic.
  3. Draft generation and store updates depend on third-party AI services and the Shopify API, including their availability and rate limits.
  4. Credit pack purchases follow Shopify Billing terms. CatalogSchema does not run a separate payment checkout.
