What counts as an operation?

An operation is each individual billable action executed, such as one AI text elevation, one JSON-LD schema injection, or one SKU assignment. It is not a fixed per-product count.

A single product can use more than one credit (for example, one elevation plus one schema injection), and a bulk batch uses one credit for every operation it runs. New operation types are billed the same way: 1 credit per operation executed.

How does the draft-first workflow work?

AI Text Elevation creates a draft description of up to 250 words from your product's existing specs, tags, and notes. The draft is held in CatalogSchema and is never published automatically.

You read, edit, and approve each draft with Review & Publish. Only approved copy is written to your Shopify store.

Does CatalogSchema edit my theme code?

No. Product facts (JSON-LD) are added through Shopify's built-in App Embed block, so your theme files are never modified. You can turn the App Embed off at any time from the Shopify theme editor.

How are credits counted?

1 operation = 1 credit. An operation is each individual billable action executed, such as one AI text elevation, one JSON-LD schema injection, or one SKU assignment. It is not a fixed per-product count, so one product or one bulk batch can use several credits depending on what runs.

You get 10 free trial credits when you install. Additional credits are purchased in-app as packs (Starter Pack: $4.99 one-time) and never expire while the app is active.

Which actions are free?

Review & Publish, Sync, View Details, and Off limits are always free and never deduct credits.

What does Off limits do?

Marking a product Off limits excludes it from elevation, schema, and SKU operations, including bulk batches, until you remove the flag. Setting or clearing Off limits is free.

How does this help as shopping shifts to AI search?

More shoppers ask AI shopping tools—not only traditional search. If product details are messy or missing, those tools may guess wrong or skip you. CatalogSchema helps quietly: clearer draft descriptions (up to 250 words) held for Review & Publish, clean price/availability/SKU facts (JSON-LD), SKU assignment, and product media detection—with zero theme-code changes, zero site slowdown, and zero migration—so tools can work from accurate store facts instead of incomplete or unstructured details.

Will this guarantee rankings, rich results, or AI citations?

Search visibility, rich results, AI citations, and traffic depend on many factors, including product descriptions, market demand, search trends, SKUs, product media, and structured data such as JSON-LD.

CatalogSchema helps organize and implement structured product information, including price, availability, and SKUs. It also supports SKU assignment, product media detection, and AI-assisted content enhancements of up to 250 words, which remain in draft status until reviewed and approved through Review & Publish.

Accurate, properly implemented structured data can help search engines understand your product information and may support eligibility for certain enhanced search features. Well-organized product information can also make relevant facts more accessible to systems that process structured data.

However, search engines and AI platforms independently determine whether, how, and when to use or display this information. CatalogSchema does not guarantee improved search rankings, rich-result display, AI citations, or increased website traffic. See the Disclaimers for additional details.

What happens when I uninstall?

Uninstalling revokes CatalogSchema's access to your store. After uninstall, and when Shopify sends the shop/redact request, we delete your stored store data, including usage and credit purchase records. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CatalogSchema ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you install and use the CatalogSchema Shopify app. We operate in accordance with Shopify's API Terms and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

  • Store metadata: shop domain, plan, and locale.
  • Product catalog fields: titles, existing descriptions, tags, vendor, product type, variants, SKUs, prices, inventory availability, and media references.
  • App usage records: operations executed (stored in shopify_copy_usage) and credit purchases (stored in shopify_credit_purchases).

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not access, collect, or store any buyer personally identifiable information (PII), such as customer names, email addresses, shipping addresses, or payment details.

4. How We Use Information

Store and product data are used only to provide the app: generating AI description drafts for your review, building JSON-LD structured data, assigning SKUs, and tracking credit usage.

5. AI Processing

Product data sent for AI text elevation is processed through third-party AI service providers. Those providers are prohibited from using your data to train external or public AI models.

6. Retention and Deletion

We retain store data while CatalogSchema is installed. When you uninstall, and when Shopify sends the shop/redact webhook, we permanently delete your store's data, including stored shopify_copy_usage and shopify_credit_purchases records.

We support Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhooks. Because we hold no buyer PII, customers/data_request and customers/redact requests involve no customer data on our side.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@catalogschema.com.
